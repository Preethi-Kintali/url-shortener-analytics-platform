# Module 03 – Step 2: DECIDE – RFC Format & Weight

## 1. Selected RFC Format
- **Choice:** **Option A: Lightweight RFC (`lightweight`)** (with progressive elaboration)

## 2. Engineering & Operational Rationale
- **Team Velocity & Agility:** A 1-to-2 page proposal can be drafted in 1–2 hours and reviewed by teammates in 10 minutes. This minimal overhead encourages engineers to propose changes early—before writing code and before becoming emotionally attached to a specific architecture.
- **Review Thoroughness & Signal:** Engineers are far more likely to read and critically engage with a concise, punchy document covering Problem, Solution, Alternatives, and Risks. Heavyweight documents frequently suffer from rubber-stamp approvals or delayed review cycles because nobody has time to read 5+ pages.
- **Scope Proportionality & Progressive Elaboration:** A lightweight RFC acts as the initial alignment gate. Once cross-functional alignment is reached on the high-level approach and rejected alternatives, the document can be progressively deepened into detailed schema contracts, migration plans, and sequence diagrams if project complexity warrants it.
