# Module 05: Stakeholder Communication — DECIDE

## Decision: Option A — Written Memo (`written_memo`)

### Strategic Rationale
A written memo provides a durable, forwardable record that stakeholders can digest at their own pace with uniform alignment across Engineering, Product, and Operations, complemented by a short Q&A session to address real-time questions.

### Key Factors Considered
1. **Stakeholder Accessibility & Scalability:** Leaders and cross-functional teams absorb complex business tradeoffs at their own pace. A memo is easily forwardable to the CFO, CTO, and product directors without requiring extensive calendar scheduling.
2. **Long-Term Referenceability:** Serves as a persistent historical artifact documenting business justification, expected costs, risks, downtime, and acceptance criteria throughout the project lifecycle.
3. **Cross-Functional Alignment:** Distributes an identical, unambiguous message across Engineering, Product, and Customer Operations, eliminating game-of-telephone communication drift.
4. **Hybrid Synthesis:** Mitigates the risk of missing real-time Q&A by using the memo as pre-read material followed by a focused live discussion for executive questions regarding budget, downtime, and timelines.
