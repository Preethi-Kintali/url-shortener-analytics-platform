# Module 02 – Step 4: VERIFY – Technical Writing Verification

## 1. Check 1: The Headline Test
- **Evaluation:** Pass.
- **Assessment:** The opening sentence (*"ShopStream will migrate its public API from REST to GraphQL over eight weeks while keeping both APIs operational."*) delivers the bottom-line decision, transition timeline, and operational constraints immediately. Priya and any engineering reader are oriented in under 5 seconds without burying the lede.

## 2. Check 2: The Longest Paragraph Test
- **Evaluation:** Pass with disciplined adherence to the One-Job Rule.
- **Assessment:** Paragraphs are maintained within 2–3 sentences. Discrete sections address distinct concerns (REST endpoint proliferation, GraphQL à la carte benefits, alternatives rejected, risks, and onboarding expectations). Dense risk matrices and multi-step action lists are formatted as scannable bullet points and numbered sequences.

## 3. Check 3: The Passive Voice Scan
- **Evaluation:** Pass with strict accountability.
- **Assessment:** Every action item in *Next Steps* and *What This Means for Priya* uses active voice with an explicit owner:
  - *Priya* reviews documentation, pairs with senior engineers, and implements an initial query.
  - *Experienced GraphQL engineers* conduct knowledge sharing and pairing.
  - *The backend team* implements query depth limits and evaluates caching.
  - *The team lead* provisions repository access and assigns onboarding milestones.
