# Module 02 – Step 2: DECIDE – Document Structure

## 1. Selected Structural Paradigm
- **Choice:** **Option A: Top-Down (`top_down`)** (Pyramid Structure with hybrid depth)

## 2. Engineering & Audience Rationale
- **Immediate Reader Orientation:** Engineering stakeholders and leaders operate under tight time constraints and have diverse technical backgrounds. Starting with the core decision, recommendation, or business outcome prevents the "buried lede" and gives readers instant clarity on why the document exists.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Once the top-level conclusion is established, the document systematically unpacks supporting context, operational pain points, technical implementation details, and architectural tradeoffs. Readers can choose their depth without risking missing the primary takeaway.
- **Hybrid Realism:** For complex investigations, RFCs, and incident postmortems, pairing a top-down executive summary at the beginning with a structured narrative analysis in the body ensures both executive efficiency and deep technical rigor.
