# Module 01 – Step 1: CONTEXT – Reflection

## 1. The Worst Feedback
- **Experience:** Receiving comments such as *"this is wrong"* or *"this is not the correct approach"* without further explanation or context.
- **Why it failed:** It created friction because it signaled a defect without diagnosing the exact mechanism of failure or pointing to how to improve. The delivery was unhelpful because identifying an error in isolation, without explaining the *why* or suggesting an alternative, leaves the author blocked and discouraged rather than empowered.

## 2. The Most Impactful Feedback
- **Experience:** Feedback that clearly articulated the underlying issue and proactively offered a constructive alternative approach.
- **Why it succeeded:** Clarifying *why* an initial design or implementation fell short transformed the critique into a durable learning moment, preventing future occurrences of the same failure mode. Actionable, specific feedback coupled with a proposed solution or guided question accelerates engineering growth and preserves team psychological safety.
